Hausa Bitcoin Initiative (HBI)

Making Bitcoin Education and Technical Knowledge Accessible to Hausa Speakers Worldwide.

About the Project

Hausa Bitcoin Initiative (HBI) is an independent, open-source educational initiative focused on making Bitcoin knowledge, technical documentation, and practical learning resources accessible to Hausa-speaking communities worldwide.

The project aims to reduce language barriers that prevent Hausa speakers from understanding Bitcoin technology and participating meaningfully in the Bitcoin ecosystem.

Our Mission

To improve access to accurate, understandable, and practical Bitcoin education through Hausa-language resources.

Our Objectives

- Translate and develop reliable Bitcoin educational materials in Hausa.
- Explain Bitcoin fundamentals and technical concepts in accessible language.
- Promote Bitcoin security, privacy, and responsible self-custody.
- Encourage open-source collaboration and community contributions.
- Help Hausa-speaking learners understand how Bitcoin works at a technical level.

Learning Resources

Our educational materials currently cover:

1. Introduction to Bitcoin
2. History and Origin of Bitcoin
3. How Bitcoin Works
4. Cryptography and Hashing in Bitcoin
5. Bitcoin Wallets and Self-Custody
6. Bitcoin Transactions and Fees
7. Bitcoin Mining and Proof of Work
8. Bitcoin Nodes and Decentralization
9. Bitcoin Security and Common Scams
10. Bitcoin and Financial Freedom

More technical guides, translations, and practical learning resources will be developed as the project grows.

Who Can Contribute?

We welcome:

- Hausa translators and language reviewers.
- Bitcoin developers and technical contributors.
- Educators and researchers.
- Writers and documentation contributors.
- Anyone interested in improving Bitcoin education in Hausa.

See "CONTRIBUTING.md" (CONTRIBUTING.md) for contribution guidelines.

Project Principles

- Accuracy: Technical information should be reliable and properly referenced.
- Openness: Educational resources should be openly accessible.
- Transparency: Contributions and project development should be visible.
- Collaboration: Community review is essential.
- Security: Responsible Bitcoin practices should be encouraged.
