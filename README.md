Hausa Bitcoin Initiative (HBI)

Making Bitcoin Knowledge Accessible to Everyone.

About the Project

Hausa Bitcoin Initiative (HBI) is an open-source educational initiative dedicated to making Bitcoin knowledge, technology, security practices, and development resources accessible to Hausa-speaking communities worldwide.

We aim to reduce language barriers in Bitcoin education by developing high-quality learning materials, practical tutorials, and technical documentation in Hausa.

Our Mission

To empower Hausa-speaking individuals with the knowledge and practical resources needed to understand, use, research, and contribute to the Bitcoin ecosystem.

What We Are Building

- Bitcoin Hausa Learning Library: Free educational materials covering Bitcoin fundamentals.
- Technical Documentation: Hausa explanations of Bitcoin architecture, transactions, mining, and consensus.
- Wallet Security Guides: Practical resources on wallet management, private keys, and personal security.
- Developer Learning Resources: Open-source guides introducing Bitcoin development.
- Community Education: Accessible learning resources for students, educators, and developers.

Our Principles

- Free and open access to knowledge.
- Open-source collaboration.
- Technical accuracy and transparency.
- Community-driven education.
- Security-conscious learning.

Project Roadmap

Phase 1: Foundation

- Establish the public repository.
- Develop the initial Bitcoin Hausa curriculum.
- Publish introductory educational materials.

Phase 2: Technical Education

- Publish Bitcoin technical guides.
- Develop practical wallet security tutorials.
- Introduce Bitcoin development concepts.

Phase 3: Community and Collaboration

- Invite contributors and reviewers.
- Improve documentation through community feedback.
- Develop additional open-source educational resources.

Contribution

We welcome educators, translators, researchers, developers, and Bitcoin enthusiasts to contribute.
