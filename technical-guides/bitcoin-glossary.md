Bitcoin Technical Glossary — Hausa

A bilingual technical glossary developed by the Hausa Bitcoin Initiative (HBI).

Our goal is to make Bitcoin terminology understandable to Hausa speakers while preserving the technical accuracy of the original concepts.

Translation Principles

1. Preserve the original technical meaning.
2. Avoid misleading literal translations.
3. Include English terms alongside Hausa explanations.
4. Verify definitions against reliable Bitcoin documentation.
5. Welcome corrections and community reviews.
