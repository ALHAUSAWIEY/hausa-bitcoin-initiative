Lesson 02: Tarihi da Asalin Bitcoin

1. Introduction

Bitcoin did not emerge overnight. It was the result of decades of research into cryptography, digital money, distributed systems, and peer-to-peer technology.

Understanding its history helps us understand why Bitcoin was created and the problems it was designed to address.

2. Before Bitcoin

Before Bitcoin, several researchers explored ways to create digital money.

Important developments included:

- DigiCash: An early digital cash system associated with David Chaum.
- b-money: A decentralized digital currency proposal by Wei Dai in 1998.
- Bit Gold: A digital currency concept proposed by Nick Szabo.
- Hashcash: A Proof-of-Work system developed by Adam Back.

These ideas contributed to the technological background from which Bitcoin emerged.

3. The Birth of Bitcoin

On October 31, 2008, an individual or group using the name Satoshi Nakamoto published a whitepaper titled:

Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System.

The paper introduced a system for electronic transactions without relying on a trusted financial intermediary.

4. The Genesis Block

On January 3, 2009, Satoshi Nakamoto mined the first Bitcoin block, known as the Genesis Block or Block 0.

It marked the beginning of the Bitcoin blockchain.

The block contained a message referencing a newspaper headline:

"The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks."

This message is widely understood as a historical reference to the financial crisis of that period.

5. The First Bitcoin Transaction

On January 12, 2009, Satoshi Nakamoto sent 10 BTC to computer scientist Hal Finney.

This was one of the earliest documented Bitcoin transactions between individuals.

6. Why Bitcoin Was Important

Bitcoin introduced several important innovations:

- Decentralized digital currency.
- Peer-to-peer electronic payments.
- Blockchain-based transaction records.
- Proof-of-Work consensus.
- A fixed maximum supply of 21 million BTC.

7. Key Takeaway

Bitcoin was not created in isolation. It built upon decades of research and introduced a practical decentralized electronic cash system.

Understanding its history helps us understand its technological and economic foundations.
