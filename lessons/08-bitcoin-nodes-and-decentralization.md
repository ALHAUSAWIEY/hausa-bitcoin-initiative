Darasi na 8: Bitcoin Nodes and Decentralization

Bitcoin Nodes da Tsarin Rarraba Iko (Decentralization)

1. Gabatarwa

Bitcoin ba ya dogara da banki guda, kamfani guda ko gwamnati guda domin gudanar da dukkan mu'amalarsa.

Yana amfani da tsarin sadarwa na kwamfutoci masu zaman kansu da ake kira Nodes.

Waɗannan Nodes suna taimakawa wajen tabbatar da cewa transactions da blocks sun bi dokokin Bitcoin.

A wannan darasi za mu fahimci yadda Nodes suke aiki, nau'o'insu da muhimmancin Decentralization.

2. Menene Bitcoin Node?

Bitcoin Node wata kwamfuta ce ko na'ura da take gudanar da manhajar Bitcoin domin shiga network da aiwatar da wasu ayyukan tabbatarwa da rarraba bayanai.

Node na iya:

- Karɓar transactions.
- Tantance transactions bisa dokokin Bitcoin.
- Karɓar blocks.
- Tantance ingancin blocks.
- Aika bayanai ga sauran Nodes.
- Taimakawa wajen kiyaye tsarin blockchain.

Ba duk Nodes ne suke yin mining ba.

3. Menene Full Node?

Full Node wata node ce da take tantance blocks da transactions bisa dokokin consensus na Bitcoin, ba tare da dogaro da miner ko wani sabis ya gaya mata cewa bayanan sun dace ba.

Bitcoin Core sanannen software ne da ake amfani da shi wajen gudanar da Full Node.

Muhimman ayyukan Full Node:

1. Tantance blocks.
2. Tantance transactions.
3. Tabbatar da bin dokokin Bitcoin.
4. Taimakawa wajen rarraba bayanai a network.
5. Taimakawa mai gudanar da ita wajen samun bayanan blockchain da kansa.

4. Menene Light Node?

Light Node ko Lightweight Client wata hanya ce ta amfani da Bitcoin network ba tare da sauke dukkan bayanan blockchain ba.

Wasu light wallets suna amfani da cikakkun Nodes na wasu mutane wajen samun bayanan transactions da blocks.

Amfaninsa:

- Ba ya buƙatar sararin ajiya mai yawa.
- Ya fi sauƙin amfani a waya.
- Ba ya buƙatar kayan aiki masu ƙarfi kamar Full Node.

Amma yana iya dogaro da wasu Nodes wajen samun bayanai, saboda haka ba ya ba mai amfani daidai matakin tantancewa mai zaman kansa kamar Full Node.

5. Bambanci Tsakanin Full Node da Light Node

Full Node| Light Node
Yana tantance blocks bisa dokokin Bitcoin| Yana amfani da bayanan da wasu Nodes suka bayar
Yana iya sauke blockchain gaba ɗaya| Ba ya buƙatar sauke dukkan blockchain
Yana buƙatar ƙarin storage| Yana buƙatar ƙaramin storage
Yana ƙara ikon mai amfani na tabbatar da bayanai da kansa| Yana iya dogaro da wasu Nodes

6. Menene Decentralization?

Decentralization na nufin rarraba iko da gudanarwa tsakanin mahalarta da dama maimakon a mayar da su ga mutum ko hukuma guda.

A Bitcoin, wannan yana nufin:

- Babu wani kamfani guda da yake mallakar network gaba ɗaya.
- Nodes daban-daban suna tantance dokokin network.
- Masu amfani suna iya gudanar da Nodes nasu.
- Canjin dokokin consensus yana buƙatar karɓuwa daga mahalarta bisa tsarin Bitcoin.

Decentralization ba yana nufin babu dokoki ba ne. Yana nufin dokokin suna gudana ne ta hanyar tsarin da mahalarta za su iya tantancewa.

7. Bambanci Tsakanin Nodes da Miners

Wannan bambanci yana da muhimmanci sosai.

Nodes:

- Suna tantance transactions da blocks.
- Suna tabbatar da cewa an bi dokokin Bitcoin.
- Ba lallai su yi mining ba.

Miners:

- Suna tattara transactions.
- Suna ƙoƙarin samar da valid Proof of Work.
- Suna ƙoƙarin ƙara sabon block cikin blockchain.

Miner yana buƙatar bin dokokin Bitcoin domin nodes su karɓi block ɗinsa.

8. Me Ya Sa Running a Full Node Yake da Muhimmanci?

Gudanar da Full Node yana ba mai amfani damar:

1. Tantance bayanan Bitcoin da kansa.
2. Rage dogaro da third-party services.
3. Taimakawa wajen rarraba bayanai a network.
4. Ƙara fahimtar yadda Bitcoin yake aiki.
5. Gudanar da wallet ko wasu kayan aiki tare da cikakken iko kan bayanan da yake tantancewa.

Full Node ba ya ba mutum ƙarin ikon mallakar Bitcoin ko ikon canza dokokin network.

9. Menene Bitcoin Core?

Bitcoin Core shi ne sanannen open-source software na gudanar da Bitcoin node.

Yana taimakawa wajen:

- Tantance transactions.
- Tantance blocks.
- Haɗawa da sauran Nodes.
- Gudanar da blockchain.
- Samar da kayan aiki ga masu amfani da masu haɓaka software.

Official website:

https://bitcoincore.org/

GitHub repository:

https://github.com/bitcoin/bitcoin
