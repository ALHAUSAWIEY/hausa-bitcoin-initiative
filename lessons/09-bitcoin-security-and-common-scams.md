Darasi na 9: Bitcoin Security and Common Scams

Tsaron Bitcoin da Hanyoyin Damfara da Ya Kamata a Guje wa

1. Gabatarwa

Bitcoin yana ba mutane damar sarrafa dukiyarsu ba tare da dogaro da banki ba. Amma wannan 'yanci yana buƙatar ilimin tsaro da kulawa.

A duniyar Bitcoin, kuskure guda ɗaya wajen kiyaye Private Key ko Seed Phrase na iya jawo asarar dukiya.

Masu amfani da Bitcoin suna fuskantar haɗurra kamar:

- Phishing.
- Fake websites.
- Fake wallet applications.
- Social engineering.
- Malware.
- Investment scams.
- Impersonation.

A wannan darasi za mu koyi yadda ake gane waɗannan haɗurra da kuma hanyoyin kariya.

2. Menene Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity shi ne ilimi da dabarun kare kwamfutoci, networks, applications da bayanai daga hare-haren yanar gizo.

A Bitcoin, cybersecurity yana taimakawa wajen:

- Kare Wallet.
- Kare Private Keys.
- Kare Seed Phrase.
- Gane shafukan damfara.
- Kare na'urori daga malware.
- Guje wa asarar dukiya.

3. Menene Phishing?

Phishing wata hanya ce ta yaudara inda maharin yake ƙoƙarin sa mutum ya bayyana bayanan sirrinsa ko ya yi wani abu da zai ba maharin damar satar dukiya.

Misali:

Wani zai iya aiko maka da saƙo yana cewa:

"Your Bitcoin wallet has a security problem. Click here to restore your wallet."

Saƙon yana iya kai ka wani website na bogi wanda yayi kama da shafin wallet na gaskiya.

Manufar maharin ita ce ya sa ka shigar da Seed Phrase ko wasu bayanan sirri.

Yadda za ka kare kanka:

1. Kada ka danna links daga mutanen da ba ka sani ba.
2. Ka bincika adireshin website da kyau.
3. Kada ka shigar da Seed Phrase a website saboda wani saƙo ya umurce ka.
4. Ka yi amfani da shafukan hukuma kawai.
5. Ka yi hattara da saƙonnin gaggawa masu razanarwa.

4. Menene Fake Websites?

Fake Website shafi ne na bogi da ake ƙirƙira domin yaudarar masu amfani.

Wani lokaci yana kama da website na wallet, exchange ko wani sanannen kamfani.

Misalan dabarun damfara:

- Canza harafi ɗaya a sunan domain.
- Amfani da tambarin kamfani ba tare da izini ba.
- Yin alƙawarin free Bitcoin.
- Neman Seed Phrase domin "verification".
- Yin kwaikwayon shafin login na gaske.

Hanyoyin kariya:

- Ka rubuta adireshin website da kanka idan kana da shi daga tushe mai inganci.
- Ka duba domain sosai.
- Ka guji shafukan da ba ka tabbatar da asalinsu ba.
- Ka yi amfani da bookmarks na shafukan hukuma.
- Ka tuna cewa HTTPS ba ya tabbatar da cewa website gaskiya ne.

5. Menene Fake Wallet Applications?

Wasu maharan suna ƙirƙirar applications na bogi da suke kwaikwayon wallet na gaskiya.

Idan mutum ya girka irin wannan app, zai iya fuskantar haɗarin satar bayanansa ko kuɗinsa.

Hanyoyin kariya:

1. Ka sauke wallet daga shafin hukuma.
2. Ka tabbatar da sunan developer.
3. Kada ka dogara da tallace-tallace kawai.
4. Ka duba bayanan app da ingancin sa kafin amfani.
5. Ka guji APK daga mutanen da ba ka amince da su ba.

6. Menene Social Engineering?

Social Engineering wata dabara ce ta amfani da yaudara, tsoro, amana ko matsin lamba domin sa mutum ya bayyana bayanan sirri ko ya yi abin da zai cutar da shi.

Misalai:

- Wani ya kira ka yana cewa shi ma'aikacin wallet company ne.
- Wani ya yi maka alƙawarin ninka Bitcoin ɗinka.
- Wani ya ce zai taimaka maka dawo da wallet idan ka ba shi Seed Phrase.
- Wani ya yi amfani da sunan abokinka ko sanannen mutum wajen neman kuɗi.

Ka tuna: Mutumin da yake da kyakkyawar niyya ba zai buƙaci Seed Phrase ko Private Key ɗinka ba.

7. Menene Malware?

Malware wata manhaja ce da aka ƙera domin yin abubuwan da za su cutar da kwamfuta ko bayanan mai amfani.

A duniyar Bitcoin, malware na iya:

- Satar bayanan sirri.
- Bibiyar abin da ake bugawa a keyboard.
- Sauya Bitcoin address da aka kwafa.
- Samun damar shiga wallet.
- Satar bayanan login.

Hanyoyin kariya:

- Ka sabunta operating system.
- Ka girka software daga tushe mai inganci.
- Kada ka buɗe fayilolin da ba ka tabbatar da asalinsu ba.
- Ka yi amfani da kariyar na'ura.
- Kada ka yi amfani da software na bogi.

8. Menene Investment Scam?

Investment Scam wata damfara ce inda ake yaudarar mutane da alƙawarin riba mai yawa ko tabbatacciyar riba domin a karɓi kuɗinsu.

Misalai:

- "Send 0.01 BTC and receive 0.1 BTC."
- "Guaranteed 20% daily profit."
- "Double your Bitcoin in 24 hours."
- Fake mining investment.
- Ponzi schemes.

Bitcoin ba ya tabbatar da ribar kasuwanci. Duk wani mutum ko kamfani da yake alƙawarin tabbatacciyar riba mai yawa yana buƙatar bincike mai zurfi.

9. Yadda Ake Kare Private Key da Seed Phrase

Private Key da Seed Phrase suna da matuƙar muhimmanci wajen kula da Bitcoin.

Ka bi waɗannan dokoki:

1. Kada ka bayyana su ga wani.
2. Kada ka tura su ta email ko social media.
3. Kada ka adana su a screenshot.
4. Kada ka shigar da su a shafin da ba ka tabbatar da shi ba.
5. Ka yi backup a wuri mai tsaro.
6. Ka kula da wanda zai iya samun damar na'urarka.
7. Kada ka yarda da mutumin da ya ce zai taimaka maka ta hanyar neman waɗannan bayanai.

Muhimmin tunatarwa: Duk wanda ya mallaki Seed Phrase zai iya samun ikon sarrafa Bitcoin da yake da alaƙa da ita.

10. Yadda Ake Tabbatar da Ingancin Wallet ko Website

Kafin ka yi amfani da wallet ko website:

- Ka samo official link daga sahihin tushe.
- Ka bincika sunan domain.
- Ka tabbatar da developer ko kamfanin da yake kula da app.
- Ka karanta takardun tsaro.
- Ka bincika tarihin sabuntawa.
- Ka guji shafukan da suke neman bayanan sirri ba tare da dalili ba.

Kada ka ɗauka cewa yawan followers ko kyakkyawan logo suna tabbatar da sahihancin project.

11. Abin Da Za Ka Yi Idan Ka Yi Zargin An Damfare Ka

1. Ka daina hulɗa da wanda ake zargi.
2. Kada ka sake aika kuɗi domin wani ya ce zai dawo maka da waɗanda aka sace.
3. Idan Seed Phrase ta bayyana, ka ɗauka cewa wallet ɗin ya lalace.
4. Idan har yanzu kana da iko da dukiyar, ka yi amfani da na'ura mai tsaro da sabon wallet mai sabbin keys domin matsar da dukiyar.
5. Ka adana shaidu kamar saƙonni, transaction IDs da website links.
6. Ka kai rahoto ga hukumomin da suka dace idan an yi zamba.

Ka tuna: Babu wani mutum da zai iya tabbatar maka cewa zai dawo da Bitcoin da aka aika zuwa ga maharin.

12. Practical Exercise

Aikin fahimta: Gane Phishing

Karanta waɗannan misalai:

Misali A:

"Wannan official wallet support ne. Aiko mana da Seed Phrase domin mu gyara matsalar account ɗinka."

Misali B:

"Congratulations! You have won 2 BTC. Send 0.01 BTC to activate your reward."

Misali C:

"Wannan shafin hukuma ne. Amma domain ɗinsa yana da ƙaramin bambanci da domain na kamfanin gaskiya."

Tambayoyi:

1. Wanne daga cikin waɗannan saƙonni yake da alamun damfara?
2. Wane irin haɗari ne kowannensu yake wakilta?
3. Me ya kamata mai amfani ya yi?
4. Waɗanne hanyoyi za a bi domin tabbatar da sahihancin website?

Kada ka danna waɗannan links ko ka aika kuɗi domin yin gwaji.

Bayan wannan darasi, dalibi ya kamata ya iya:

- Fahimtar muhimmancin cybersecurity a Bitcoin.

- Fahimtar social engineering.

- Guje wa investment scams.
- Kare Private Key da Seed Phrase.
- Sanin matakan da za a ɗauka idan an yi zargin satar wallet.
