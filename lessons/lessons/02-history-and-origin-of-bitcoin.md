Darasi na 2: Tarihi da Asalin Bitcoin

Lesson 2: History and Origin of Bitcoin

2.1 Gabatarwa

Bitcoin bai bayyana a cikin dare ɗaya ba. Ƙirƙirarsa ta samo asali ne daga dogon bincike kan cryptography, digital money da hanyoyin aika kuɗi ta intanet ba tare da dogaro da wata hukuma guda ɗaya ba.

Domin fahimtar Bitcoin, yana da muhimmanci mu fahimci matsalolin da suka gabace shi.

2.2 Ƙoƙarin ƙirƙirar kuɗin dijital kafin Bitcoin

Kafin shekarar 2009, masu bincike sun yi ƙoƙarin ƙirƙirar kuɗin dijital.

Wasu muhimman misalai sun haɗa da:

- DigiCash: Tsarin kuɗin lantarki da David Chaum ya ƙirƙira.
- Hashcash: Tsarin Proof of Work da Adam Back ya gabatar a shekarar 1997.
- b-money: Shawarar kuɗin dijital da Wei Dai ya wallafa a shekarar 1998.
- Bit Gold: Tsarin kuɗin dijital da Nick Szabo ya gabatar.

Waɗannan bincike sun taimaka wajen samar da wasu daga cikin ra'ayoyin fasaha da suka shafi Bitcoin.

2.3 Matsalar Double Spending

Ɗaya daga cikin manyan ƙalubalen kuɗin dijital shi ne Double Spending.

Wannan na nufin mutum ya yi ƙoƙarin amfani da kuɗin dijital guda ɗaya wajen biyan mutane biyu daban-daban.

A tsarin kuɗin gargajiya, banki kan taimaka wajen tabbatar da cewa ba a kashe kuɗi iri ɗaya sau biyu ba.

Bitcoin ya samar da wata hanya ta rarraba tabbatar da hada-hadar kuɗi ta hanyar nodes, blockchain da Proof of Work.

2.4 Satoshi Nakamoto da Bitcoin Whitepaper

A ranar 31 Oktoba 2008, wani mutum ko ƙungiya mai amfani da sunan Satoshi Nakamoto ya wallafa takardar bincike mai suna:

Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System.

Takardar ta bayyana tsarin aika kuɗin lantarki kai tsaye daga mutum zuwa mutum ba tare da buƙatar amintaccen matsakaici ba.

Ba a tabbatar da ainihin wanda ke bayan sunan Satoshi Nakamoto ba har yanzu.

2.5 Bitcoin Genesis Block

A ranar 3 Janairu 2009, an ƙirƙiri block na farko a blockchain na Bitcoin.

Ana kiran wannan block da Genesis Block ko Block 0.

A cikin wannan block akwai saƙon da ya ambaci wani kanun labarin jaridar The Times game da shirin ceto bankuna a lokacin rikicin kuɗi na duniya.

Wannan saƙon ya zama wani muhimmin ɓangare na tarihin Bitcoin.

2.6 Transaction na farko tsakanin mutane

A ranar 12 Janairu 2009, an gudanar da ɗaya daga cikin muhimman hada-hadar Bitcoin na farko tsakanin Satoshi Nakamoto da Hal Finney.

Wannan ya nuna cewa tsarin Bitcoin yana iya aika ƙima daga mutum zuwa wani.

2.7 Bitcoin Pizza Day

A ranar 22 Mayu 2010, Laszlo Hanyecz ya yi amfani da Bitcoin 10,000 wajen samun pizzas biyu.

Wannan lamari ya zama sananne da Bitcoin Pizza Day.

Yana daga cikin shahararrun abubuwan tarihi da ke nuna yadda aka fara amfani da Bitcoin wajen sayen kaya da ayyuka.

2.9 Me ya sa tarihin Bitcoin yake da muhimmanci?

Tarihin Bitcoin yana taimaka mana fahimtar cewa fasahar zamani ba ta tasowa daga babu komai.

Sabbin ƙirƙirarru suna amfani da bincike, gwaje-gwaje da tunanin waɗanda suka gabata.

Bitcoin ya haɗa ra'ayoyi daga cryptography, distributed systems, economics da computer science domin samar da sabon tsarin kuɗin dijital.
