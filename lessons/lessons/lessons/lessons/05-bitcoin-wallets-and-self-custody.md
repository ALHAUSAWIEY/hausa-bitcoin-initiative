Darasi na 5: Bitcoin Wallets and Self-Custody

Wallets na Bitcoin da Yadda Ake Kula da Dukiyarka da Kanka

1. Gabatarwa

Bitcoin yana ba mutum damar mallakar da sarrafa kuɗinsa ba tare da dogaro da banki ko wani kamfani ba.

Amma wannan 'yanci yana zuwa da nauyin kula da tsaron dukiya.

A wannan darasi za mu fahimci yadda Bitcoin Wallet yake aiki, menene Private Key, Seed Phrase, da kuma yadda ake kare Bitcoin daga sata ko asara.

2. Menene Bitcoin Wallet?

Bitcoin Wallet wata manhaja ce ko na'ura da ake amfani da ita wajen sarrafa mabuɗan da ke ba mutum damar amfani da Bitcoin.

Wallet ba ya adana Bitcoin a cikinsa kamar yadda jaka take adana kuɗi.

Bitcoin yana nan a blockchain. Wallet yana taimakawa wajen sarrafa Private Keys da ƙirƙirar transactions.

Misalan Wallets sun haɗa da:

- Mobile Wallets
- Desktop Wallets
- Hardware Wallets
- Paper Wallets

3. Nau'o'in Bitcoin Wallets

A. Mobile Wallets

Wallets ne da ake amfani da su a wayoyin hannu.

Misalai:

- Nunchuk
- BlueWallet
- Blockstream Green

Amfaninsu:

- Sauƙin amfani.
- Ana iya amfani da su a wurare daban-daban.
- Sun dace da masu farawa.

B. Desktop Wallets

Wallets ne da ake girkawa a kwamfuta.

Misalai:

- Sparrow Wallet
- Bitcoin Core Wallet

Amfaninsu:

- Suna ba da damar ƙarin bincike da sarrafa transactions.
- Wasu suna ba da cikakken ikon kula da bayanan wallet.

C. Hardware Wallets

Waɗannan na'urori ne da aka ƙera musamman domin kare Private Keys daga haɗarin kwamfuta ko waya.

Misalai:

- Trezor
- Coldcard
- Ledger

Amfaninsu:

- Suna taimakawa wajen kiyaye Private Keys a na'ura ta musamman.
- Suna rage haɗarin satar mabuɗi ta hanyar malware idan an yi amfani da su daidai.

D. Paper Wallets

Wannan hanya ce ta rubuta ko buga bayanan mabuɗin wallet a takarda.

A yau, amfani da Paper Wallet na gargajiya yana da haɗari ga masu farawa saboda kuskuren ƙirƙira, amfani ko adanawa na iya jawo asarar dukiya.

4. Menene Custodial da Non-Custodial Wallet?

Custodial Wallet

Wallet ne inda wani kamfani ko mai bada sabis yake kula da Private Keys a madadin mai amfani.

Misali, wasu account na crypto exchanges suna amfani da wannan tsarin.

Idan mai amfani ya rasa damar shiga account, zai iya dogaro da tsarin kamfanin wajen dawo da shi.

Non-Custodial Wallet

Wallet ne inda mai amfani yake kula da Private Keys da kansa.

Wannan yana ba mutum ƙarin iko, amma yana nufin shi ne ke da alhakin kare mabuɗansa.

5. Menene Self-Custody?

Self-Custody na nufin mutum ya kula da Private Keys ɗinsa da kansa maimakon ya dogara da wani kamfani.

A Bitcoin, wannan yana nufin kana da ikon sanya hannu a kan transactions ɗinka ba tare da wani mai kula da mabuɗin a madadinka ba.

Muhimmin tunani:

Not your keys, not your coins.

Wannan magana tana tunatar da masu Bitcoin cewa idan ba su da ikon kula da Private Keys, suna dogaro da wani wajen sarrafa dukiyar.

6. Menene Seed Phrase?

Seed Phrase jerin kalmomi ne da ake amfani da su wajen dawo da wallet ko sake samar da mabuɗan sa.

Wasu wallets suna amfani da kalmomi 12, 18 ko 24 bisa tsarin da suka zaɓa.

Seed Phrase tana da matuƙar muhimmanci saboda wanda ya mallake ta zai iya samun damar sarrafa dukiyar da take da alaƙa da ita.

Ka kiyaye waɗannan dokoki:

1. Kada ka aika Seed Phrase ga kowa.
2. Kada ka saka ta a WhatsApp ko Telegram.
3. Kada ka ɗauki screenshot ɗinta.
4. Kada ka shigar da ita a website saboda wani ya ce maka ka yi haka.
5. Ka rubuta ta a hanya mai tsaro kuma ka kare ta daga wuta, ruwa da sata.
6. Kada ka adana ta a wurin da kowa zai iya samu.

7. Hot Wallet da Cold Wallet

Hot Wallet

Wallet ne da yake da alaƙa da internet a lokacin amfani.

Misalai: Wallet na waya ko kwamfuta da ake amfani da shi wajen gudanar da transactions.

Amfani:

- Sauƙin amfani.
- Ya dace da ƙananan kuɗin da ake kashewa akai-akai.

Haɗari:

- Zai iya fuskantar malware, phishing da satar bayanai.

Cold Wallet

Hanya ce ta adana Private Keys ba tare da kasancewarsu a kan na'urar da ke haɗe da internet kai tsaye ba.

Hardware Wallet na iya taimakawa wajen aiwatar da wannan tsarin.

Amfani:

- Ya dace da adana dukiyar da ba a son amfani da ita akai-akai.
- Yana rage wasu haɗurran da ke tattare da kasancewar mabuɗi a online.

8. Yadda Ake Kare Bitcoin Wallet

Tsaron wallet yana farawa ne daga halayen mai amfani.

Muhimman matakai:

- Yi amfani da wallet daga shafin hukuma ko ingantaccen tushe.
- Tabbatar da sunan app kafin ka girka shi.
- Yi amfani da password mai ƙarfi da kariyar na'ura.
- Ka kiyaye Seed Phrase da Private Key.
- Ka guji links da saƙonnin da ba ka tabbatar da asalinsu ba.
- Ka fara gwada sabon wallet da ƙaramin adadi.
- Ka tabbatar da address kafin aika Bitcoin.
- Ka fahimci cewa transactions da aka tabbatar a Bitcoin yawanci ba za a iya soke su ba.

9. Backup and Recovery

Backup yana taimakawa wajen dawo da damar amfani da wallet idan waya ko kwamfuta ta lalace ko ta ɓace.

Seed Phrase na iya zama hanyar dawo da wallet idan an samar da ita bisa tsarin da ya dace.

Amma ka tuna:

- Backup ba ya maye gurbin tsaro.
- Idan wani ya samu Seed Phrase, zai iya sarrafa dukiyar.
- Kada ka gwada recovery da babban adadin Bitcoin ba tare da fahimtar abin da kake yi ba.

10. Practical Exercise

Aikin fahimta:

1. Bincika bambanci tsakanin Custodial da Non-Custodial Wallet.
2. Bincika bambanci tsakanin Hot Wallet da Cold Wallet.
3. Ka zaɓi wani sanannen Bitcoin wallet daga shafin hukuma.
4. Karanta bayanin tsaronsa da yadda yake kula da Private Keys.
5. Rubuta abubuwa biyar da za ka yi domin kare wallet ɗinka.

Muhimmin gargaɗi: Kada ka ƙirƙiri wallet na gwaji da kuɗin gaske ko ka bayyana Seed Phrase a lokacin wannan darasi. Kada ka taɓa aika mana da Seed Phrase ko Private Key.
