Darasi na 6: Bitcoin Transactions and Fees

Yadda Bitcoin Transactions da Kuɗin Aika Bitcoin Suke Aiki

1. Gabatarwa

Bitcoin yana ba mutane damar aikawa da karɓar kuɗi ta hanyar network ba tare da buƙatar banki ya tabbatar da kowace mu'amala ba.

Amma kafin a fahimci yadda ake aika Bitcoin, dole ne mu fahimci yadda transaction yake samuwa, yadda network yake tantance shi, da kuma yadda ake biyan kuɗin transaction.

A wannan darasi za mu koyi:

- Menene Bitcoin Transaction?
- Yadda transaction yake aiki.
- Menene Transaction ID (TXID)?
- Menene Mempool?
- Menene Transaction Fees?
- Menene Confirmations?
- Yadda ake bincika transaction a Blockchain Explorer.

2. Menene Bitcoin Transaction?

Bitcoin Transaction ita ce mu'amalar da ake amfani da ita wajen aika Bitcoin daga wani adireshi zuwa wani.

Misali:

Aisha tana son aika 0.001 BTC zuwa ga Musa.

Aisha za ta yi amfani da wallet ɗinta wajen ƙirƙirar transaction, ta sanya adireshin Musa da adadin Bitcoin da take son aikawa, sannan wallet ɗinta zai shirya transaction ɗin domin a sanya masa Digital Signature.

Transaction ɗin zai shiga network domin nodes su tantance shi.

Muhimmi: Bitcoin ba ya aiki kamar aika saƙon banki kawai. Tsarin transaction yana dogara ne da dokokin blockchain da tsarin UTXO.

3. Menene UTXO?

UTXO na nufin Unspent Transaction Output.

Wato wani ɓangare ne na Bitcoin da aka samu daga transaction amma ba a kashe shi ba tukuna.

Bitcoin yana amfani da tsarin UTXO wajen bin diddigin kuɗin da za a iya kashewa.

Misali:

- Kana da UTXO mai darajar 0.01 BTC.
- Kana son aika 0.003 BTC.
- Za a iya amfani da wannan UTXO wajen biyan 0.003 BTC da transaction fee.
- Sauran kuɗin da suka rage za su iya dawowa gare ka a matsayin change output.

Ba lallai wallet ya aika UTXO guda ɗaya ba. Zai iya haɗa UTXOs da dama domin samar da adadin da ake buƙata.

4. Yadda Bitcoin Transaction Yake Aiki

Ga matakan da transaction yake bi:

Mataki na 1: Ƙirƙirar Transaction

Mai amfani ya zaɓi:

- Adadin Bitcoin da zai aika.
- Adireshin wanda zai karɓa.
- Transaction fee.

Mataki na 2: Digital Signature

Wallet yana amfani da Private Key da ya dace wajen sanya hannu a kan transaction.

Wannan yana tabbatar da cewa an samu izinin kashe UTXOs ɗin da aka zaɓa.

Mataki na 3: Broadcast

Wallet yana aika transaction ɗin zuwa Bitcoin network.

Mataki na 4: Validation

Bitcoin nodes suna tantance transaction bisa dokokin network.

Suna duba abubuwa kamar:

- Ingancin Digital Signature.
- Ko UTXOs ɗin da aka yi amfani da su suna nan kuma ba a kashe su ba.
- Ko transaction ɗin ya bi dokokin Bitcoin.

Mataki na 5: Mempool

Idan transaction ya dace da dokokin node, zai iya shiga mempool ɗinsa.

Mataki na 6: Mining

Miner zai iya zaɓar transaction daga mempool ya saka shi cikin block.

Mataki na 7: Confirmation

Idan block ɗin ya shiga blockchain, transaction ɗin ya samu confirmation na farko.

5. Menene Transaction ID (TXID)?

TXID na nufin Transaction Identifier.

Wani Hash ne da ake amfani da shi wajen gano transaction a Bitcoin blockchain.

Ana iya amfani da TXID wajen:

- Bincika transaction.
- Duba adadin Bitcoin da aka aika.
- Duba adireshin da aka aika zuwa gare shi.
- Duba matsayin confirmation.
- Bincika transaction fee.

TXID ba Private Key ba ne. Amma saboda bayanan Bitcoin suna bayyane a blockchain, ya kamata a fahimci cewa binciken transaction na iya bayyana bayanan mu'amalar da aka yi.

6. Menene Mempool?

Mempool na nufin wurin da Bitcoin nodes suke ajiye transactions masu inganci waɗanda ba a saka su cikin block ba tukuna.

Muhimman abubuwa:

- Kowace node tana da nata mempool.
- Ba lallai mempool na duk nodes su kasance iri ɗaya ba.
- Transaction da yake cikin mempool bai samu confirmation ba tukuna.
- Miners suna zaɓar transactions bisa dokokin network da abubuwan da suka fi dacewa da su.

Saboda haka, kasancewar transaction a mempool ba yana nufin an kammala shi ba.

7. Menene Bitcoin Transaction Fee?

Transaction Fee shi ne kuɗin da mai aikawa yake bayarwa domin transaction ɗinsa ya shiga block.

A Bitcoin, miners suna samun transaction fees daga transactions da suke cikin block ɗin da suka haƙa.

Muhimmi:

Transaction fee ba ya dogara kawai da adadin Bitcoin da ake aikawa.

Yawanci yana dogara ne da girman transaction a bytes ko virtual bytes (vBytes) da kuma fee rate da aka zaɓa.

Ana auna fee rate da satoshis per virtual byte (sat/vB).

Misali:

Idan transaction ɗinka yana da girman 150 vBytes kuma ka zaɓi 20 sat/vB:

150 × 20 = 3,000 satoshis.

Wannan misali ne kawai, ba farashin fee na yanzu ba.

8. Me Ya Sa Transaction Fees Suke Sauyawa?

Transaction fees suna iya canzawa saboda:

1. Yawan transactions da suke jiran confirmation.
2. Girman transaction.
3. Fee rate da masu amfani suka zaɓa.
4. Sararin da yake cikin block.

Lokacin da mutane da yawa suke son transactions ɗinsu su shiga blocks cikin gaggawa, gasar neman block space na iya ƙara fee rate.

9. Menene Confirmation?

Confirmation yana nufin an saka transaction cikin block da aka ƙara a blockchain.

- 0 confirmations: Ba a saka transaction cikin block ba tukuna.
- 1 confirmation: An saka shi cikin block na farko.
- 2 confirmations: An ƙara wani block a bayansa.
- 6 confirmations: Akwai blocks shida daga block ɗin da transaction ɗin yake ciki, ciki har da block ɗin.

Ƙarin confirmations yana ƙara wahalar sauya tarihin transaction.

Amma babu adadin confirmations guda ɗaya da ya dace da kowane transaction. Bukatar confirmation tana dogara da darajar transaction da haɗarin da ake son kaucewa.

10. Menene Blockchain Explorer?

Blockchain Explorer wani kayan aiki ne da ake amfani da shi wajen duba bayanan Bitcoin blockchain.

Misalai:

- Mempool.space: https://mempool.space/
- Blockstream Explorer: https://blockstream.info/

Za ka iya amfani da su wajen bincika:

- Transaction ID.
- Block height.
- Transaction fees.
- Confirmations.
- Addresses.
- Mempool status.
