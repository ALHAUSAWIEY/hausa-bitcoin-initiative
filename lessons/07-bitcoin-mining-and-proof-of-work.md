Darasi na 7: Bitcoin Mining and Proof of Work

Bitcoin Mining da Tsarin Tabbatar da Aiki (Proof of Work)

1. Gabatarwa

Bitcoin yana amfani da wata fasaha ta musamman wajen tabbatar da transactions da kare tsarin blockchain. Wannan fasaha ita ce Proof of Work (PoW).

A cikin wannan tsarin, akwai wasu mahalarta da ake kira Miners. Suna amfani da na'urorin kwamfuta wajen yin lissafin da zai ba su damar ƙara sabon block cikin blockchain.

Mining ba kawai hanyar samun Bitcoin ba ce. Yana taimakawa wajen:

- Tabbatar da transactions.
- Haɗa sabbin blocks cikin blockchain.
- Kare network daga wasu hare-hare.
- Samar da tsarin da mahalarta za su iya amincewa da shi ba tare da babban jami'in tsakiya ba.

2. Menene Bitcoin Mining?

Bitcoin Mining wani aiki ne da ake amfani da shi wajen samar da sabbin blocks da kuma kare tsarin Bitcoin ta hanyar Proof of Work.

Miners suna:

1. Karɓar transactions daga network.
2. Tantance su bisa dokokin Bitcoin.
3. Zaɓar transactions domin saka su cikin block.
4. Shirya block header.
5. Yin ƙoƙarin nemo hash da ya cika sharadin difficulty.
6. Watsa block ɗin da suka samu zuwa network.

Muhimmi: Miners ba su da ikon canza dokokin Bitcoin yadda suke so. Nodes suna tantance blocks bisa dokokin consensus na Bitcoin.

3. Menene Proof of Work?

Proof of Work wata hanya ce ta tabbatar da cewa an yi wani aiki na lissafi kafin a samu damar ƙara sabon block.

A Bitcoin, miners suna amfani da SHA-256 hashing wajen ƙoƙarin nemo block hash da yake ƙasa ko daidai da target da network ya ƙayyade.

Ba a san wace nonce za ta samar da sakamakon da ake buƙata ba, saboda haka miners suna gwada lambobi daban-daban akai-akai.

Wannan yana buƙatar:

- Ƙarfin sarrafa bayanai.
- Wutar lantarki.
- Na'urorin mining.
- Lokaci da albarkatu.

4. Yadda Mining Yake Aiki

Ga matakan mining:

Mataki na 1: Tarin Transactions

Miners suna tattara transactions da suka dace daga mempool.

Mataki na 2: Gina Block

Ana shirya block wanda yake ɗauke da transactions da bayanan block header.

Mataki na 3: Hashing

Ana amfani da SHA-256 sau biyu wajen samar da block hash daga block header.

Mataki na 4: Gwajin Nonce

Miners suna sauya nonce da sauran bayanan da za a iya sauyawa domin gwada hashes daban-daban.

Mataki na 5: Samun Valid Proof of Work

Idan miner ya sami hash da ya cika sharadin target, zai watsa block ɗin zuwa network.

Mataki na 6: Tantance Block

Nodes suna duba ko block ɗin ya bi dokokin Bitcoin.

Idan ya dace, nodes za su iya karɓarsa su ƙara shi cikin blockchain ɗinsu.

5. Menene Mining Difficulty?

Mining Difficulty yana nufin matakin wahalar nemo valid block hash.

Bitcoin yana da tsarin da yake daidaita difficulty bayan kowane blocks 2,016.

Manufar wannan daidaitawa ita ce kiyaye matsakaicin lokacin samar da block kusan mintuna 10.

Idan yawan ƙarfin mining a network ya ƙaru, difficulty na iya ƙaruwa.

Idan ƙarfin mining ya ragu, difficulty na iya raguwa.

Wannan yana taimakawa wajen daidaita samar da blocks duk da sauyin ƙarfin mining.

6. Menene Block Reward?

Block Reward shi ne sabon Bitcoin da ake bayarwa ga miner da ya samar da valid block, tare da transaction fees na transactions da ke cikin block ɗin.

Block subsidy yana raguwa ta hanyar wani tsari da ake kira Halving.

A halving, ana rage subsidy zuwa rabinsa bayan kowane blocks 210,000.

Wannan yana daga cikin hanyoyin da Bitcoin yake sarrafa samar da sabbin Bitcoin.

7. Menene Bitcoin Halving?

Halving wani muhimmin tsari ne a cikin dokokin Bitcoin wanda yake rage block subsidy da kashi 50%.

Tarihin Halving:

- 2009: An fara da 50 BTC a kowane block.
- 2012: An rage zuwa 25 BTC.
- 2016: An rage zuwa 12.5 BTC.
- 2020: An rage zuwa 6.25 BTC.
- 2024: An rage zuwa 3.125 BTC.

Halving yana faruwa bayan kowane blocks 210,000, ba bisa takamaiman ranar kalanda ba.

8. Menene Mining Pool?

Mining Pool haɗin gwiwa ne tsakanin miners da suke haɗa ƙarfin mining ɗinsu domin ƙara damar samun block reward.

Idan pool ya sami block, ana raba ladan bisa tsarin da pool ya tanada da gudummawar mining da aka bayar.

Amfaninsa:

- Rage rashin tabbas na samun lada.
- Samar da ƙarin daidaiton samun kuɗin shiga ga mahalarta.

Amma shiga mining pool ba ya nufin mutum ya mallaki dukkan Bitcoin da pool ya samu.

9. Menene ASIC Miner?

ASIC na nufin Application-Specific Integrated Circuit.

Wata na'ura ce da aka ƙera domin yin wani aiki na musamman cikin sauri da inganci.

A Bitcoin mining, ana amfani da ASIC miners domin yin SHA-256 hashing.

Sun fi kwamfutocin yau da kullum dacewa da aikin Bitcoin mining saboda an ƙera su musamman domin wannan aiki.

10. Me Ya Sa Proof of Work Yake da Muhimmanci?

Proof of Work yana taimakawa wajen:

- Kare tarihin transactions.
- Samar da gasa wajen samar da blocks.
- Ƙara wahalar sauya tsoffin blocks.
- Rage yiwuwar kashe Bitcoin sau biyu (Double Spending).
- Ba da damar network ya ci gaba ba tare da babban mai kula da shi ba.

Idan wani yana son sauya block na baya, zai buƙaci sake yin aikin Proof of Work da kuma yin gogayya da sauran ƙarfin mining na network.

11. Menene 51% Attack?

51% Attack wani yanayi ne inda wani mahaluki ko ƙungiya ta samu rinjaye a kan fiye da rabin total hash rate na network.

Irin wannan rinjaye zai iya ƙara damar yin wasu ayyuka kamar:

- Reorganizing wasu tsoffin blocks.
- Yin double-spending na transactions ɗin da suke sarrafawa.

Amma ba ya ba maharin ikon:

- Ƙirƙirar Bitcoin fiye da dokokin network.
- Sanya invalid transactions su zama valid.
- Satar Bitcoin daga wallet kawai ta hanyar mallakar hash rate.
