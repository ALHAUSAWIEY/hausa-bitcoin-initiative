Darasi na 3: Yadda Bitcoin Yake Aiki

Lesson 3: How Bitcoin Works

3.1 Gabatarwa

Bitcoin yana aiki ne ta hanyar haɗin fasahohi da dama, ciki har da cryptography, peer-to-peer networking, distributed ledger da consensus mechanism.

Waɗannan fasahohi suna ba mutane damar aika da karɓar Bitcoin ba tare da buƙatar banki ya tabbatar da kowace transaction ba.

3.2 Menene Bitcoin Network?

Bitcoin Network ita ce hanyar sadarwa ta kwamfutoci da ke gudanar da software na Bitcoin.

Waɗannan kwamfutoci ana kiransu Nodes.

Ayyukansu sun haɗa da:

- Karɓar transactions.
- Binciken ingancin transactions.
- Rarraba bayanai ga sauran nodes.
- Tabbatar da bin ƙa'idojin Bitcoin.

3.3 Menene Bitcoin Wallet?

Bitcoin wallet manhaja ce ko na'ura da ke taimakawa wajen sarrafa makullan sirri da ake amfani da su wajen aika Bitcoin.

Muhimmin abu: Bitcoin ba ya zama a cikin wallet kamar yadda takarda ke zama a cikin jaka. Bayanan mallaka da transactions suna cikin blockchain; wallet yana sarrafa makullan da ke ba mai amfani damar amfani da Bitcoin.

Nau'o'in wallets sun haɗa da:

- Mobile wallets.
- Desktop wallets.
- Hardware wallets.
- Paper backups.

3.4 Public Key da Private Key

Bitcoin yana amfani da cryptographic keys.

Private Key: Sirrin lamba ne da ke ba mutum damar sanya hannu kan transaction. Kada a taɓa bai wa wani.

Public Key: Ana samar da shi daga private key, kuma yana taimakawa wajen tabbatar da digital signature.

Bitcoin Address: Wannan shi ne adireshin da ake amfani da shi wajen karɓar Bitcoin. Ana samar da shi ta amfani da tsarin cryptography daga public key ko wasu bayanan script.

Kada a ruɗe public key da Bitcoin address; ba abu ɗaya ba ne.

3.5 Yadda Bitcoin Transaction yake aiki

Misali, Aisha tana son aika Bitcoin ga Musa.

1. Aisha ta buɗe wallet ɗinta.
2. Ta saka Bitcoin address na Musa.
3. Ta zaɓi adadin Bitcoin da za ta aika.
4. Wallet ɗinta ya ƙirƙiri transaction.
5. Ta sanya hannu da private key ɗinta.
6. Transaction ɗin ya shiga Bitcoin network.
7. Nodes suna bincika ko ya cika ƙa'idojin Bitcoin.
8. Miners za su iya haɗa shi cikin block.
9. Bayan an haɗa transaction ɗin cikin block, ana iya samun ƙarin confirmations daga blocks da suka biyo baya.

3.6 Menene Mempool?

Mempool wani wurin jiran transactions ne da nodes suka karɓa amma ba a saka su cikin block ba tukuna.

Mempool ba babban database guda ɗaya ba ne; kowane node yana da nasa tsarin adana transactions masu jiran tabbatarwa.

3.7 Menene Mining?

Bitcoin mining wani aiki ne da miners ke amfani da kwamfutoci wajen yin ƙoƙarin nemo block hash da ya cika sharuddan Proof of Work.

Ayyukansa sun haɗa da:

- Ƙoƙarin samar da ingantaccen block.
- Tabbatar da bin ƙa'idojin Bitcoin.
- Kare hanyar sadarwa ta hanyar Proof of Work.
- Samun block subsidy da transaction fees idan an samu block mai inganci.

3.8 Menene Block?

Block wani sashe ne na bayanan blockchain.

Yana ƙunshe da:

- Block header.
- Transactions.
- Previous block hash.
- Merkle root.
- Timestamp.
- Difficulty target da nonce.

Blocks suna haɗuwa ta hanyar cryptographic hashes, wanda ke samar da blockchain.

3.9 Menene Confirmations?

Confirmation yana faruwa ne idan transaction ya shiga block da aka karɓa cikin blockchain.

Duk lokacin da aka ƙara sabon block bayan block ɗin transaction, adadin confirmations yana ƙaruwa.

Ƙarin confirmations kan ƙara wahalar sake tsara tarihin transaction, amma ba su zama cikakken tabbacin cewa ba za a taɓa samun reorganization ba.

3.10 Yadda tsarin yake haɗuwa

Wallet → Transaction → Bitcoin Nodes → Mempool → Miner → Block → Blockchain.

Wannan shi ne sauƙaƙƙen bayani na yadda transaction zai iya tafiya cikin Bitcoin network.
