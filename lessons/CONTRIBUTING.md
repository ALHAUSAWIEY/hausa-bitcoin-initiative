Contributing to Hausa Bitcoin Initiative

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Hausa Bitcoin Initiative (HBI).

Our goal is to make reliable Bitcoin education accessible to Hausa-speaking communities worldwide.

Who Can Contribute?

We welcome:

- Hausa translators.
- Bitcoin educators.
- Software developers.
- Technical writers.
- Researchers.
- Community reviewers.

How to Contribute

1. Explore the existing educational materials.
2. Identify errors, missing explanations, or opportunities for improvement.
3. Fork this repository.
4. Create a new branch for your contribution.
5. Submit a pull request describing your changes.

Contribution Guidelines

- Ensure technical accuracy.
- Use clear and understandable Hausa.
- Reference reliable sources.
- Avoid misleading financial claims.
- Respect open-source principles.
- Keep educational materials accessible to everyone.

Our Commitment

Every contribution should help improve the quality, accessibility, and reliability of Bitcoin education.

Thank you for building with us.
