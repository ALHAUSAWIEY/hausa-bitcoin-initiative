Darasi na 1: Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin

Lesson 1: Introduction to Bitcoin

1.1 Menene Bitcoin? | What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin kuɗi ne na dijital (digital currency) wanda yake ba mutane damar aika da karɓar ƙima ta hanyar intanet ba tare da buƙatar banki ko wani babban jami'in kula da hada-hadar kuɗi a matsayin matsakaici ba.

Bitcoin yana amfani da fasahar Blockchain, wadda ke taimakawa wajen adana tarihin hada-hadar kuɗi a cikin hanyar sadarwa ta kwamfutoci da dama.

Bitcoin ba mallakar gwamnati, banki ko kamfani guda ɗaya ba ne. Yana aiki ne ta hanyar tsarin buɗaɗɗen tushe (open-source) da kuma hanyar sadarwa mai rarraba iko (decentralized network).

1.2 Wanene ya ƙirƙiri Bitcoin?

An ƙaddamar da Bitcoin a shekarar 2009 ta wani mutum ko ƙungiya da ke amfani da sunan Satoshi Nakamoto.

Har yanzu ba a tabbatar da ainihin wanda ke bayan wannan suna ba.

A shekarar 2008, Satoshi ya wallafa takardar bincike mai suna:

Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System.

Wannan takarda ta bayyana yadda mutane za su iya aika kuɗin dijital kai tsaye ga juna ba tare da dogaro da wani matsakaici ba.

1.3 Me ya sa aka ƙirƙiri Bitcoin?

Babban manufar Bitcoin ita ce samar da tsarin kuɗin lantarki da mutane za su iya amfani da shi kai tsaye tsakanin junansu.

Wasu muhimman matsalolin da tsarin Bitcoin yake ƙoƙarin magancewa sun haɗa da:

- Dogaro da cibiyar kuɗi guda ɗaya.
- Matsalar kashe kuɗi iri ɗaya sau biyu (Double Spending).
- Bukatar amintaccen tsarin tabbatar da hada-hadar kuɗi.
- Buƙatar tsarin kuɗi mai buɗaɗɗen tushe.

1.4 Muhimman siffofin Bitcoin

1. Decentralization — Rarraba iko

Babu wata hukuma guda ɗaya da ke da cikakken iko kan hanyar sadarwar Bitcoin.

2. Transparency — Bayyananniyar hanya

Ana iya duba bayanan hada-hadar Bitcoin a cikin blockchain na jama'a.

3. Security — Tsaro

Bitcoin yana amfani da cryptography, digital signatures da tsarin consensus domin kare hanyar sadarwarsa.

4. Limited Supply — Ƙayyadadden adadi

An tsara Bitcoin domin kada adadinsa ya wuce BTC miliyan 21.

5. Open Source — Buɗaɗɗen tushe

Ana iya duba lambar software ɗinsa, yin nazari da bayar da gudummawar inganta shi bisa lasisinsa.

1.5 Yadda Bitcoin yake aiki

A taƙaice, tsarin yana aiki kamar haka:

1. Mai amfani ya ƙirƙiri transaction.
2. Ya sanya hannu ta amfani da private key.
3. Ana watsa transaction ɗin zuwa nodes.
4. Nodes suna bincika ingancinsa.
5. Miners suna haɗa transactions cikin block ta hanyar Proof of Work.
6. Sabon block yana shiga blockchain idan ya cika sharuddan tsarin.

1.6 Shin Bitcoin da Blockchain abu ɗaya ne?

A'a.

- Bitcoin: Wani tsarin kuɗin dijital ne da yake amfani da blockchain.
- Blockchain: Fasaha ce ta adana bayanai a cikin jerin blocks da ke da alaƙa da juna ta hanyar cryptography.

Bitcoin yana ɗaya daga cikin shahararrun aikace-aikacen blockchain.

1.7 Amfanin Bitcoin

- Aika da karɓar kuɗi ta intanet.
- Samun damar shiga tsarin kuɗi ba tare da asusun banki na gargajiya ba.
- Buɗaɗɗen tsarin biyan kuɗi.
- Samun damar binciken bayanan transaction a fili.

1.8 Ƙalubalen Bitcoin

- Sauyin farashi (Volatility).
- Haɗarin rasa private keys.
- Zamba da phishing.
- Canjin kuɗin transaction da saurin tabbatarwa.
- Dokoki da ƙa'idoji sun bambanta daga ƙasa zuwa ƙasa.

Bitcoin ba shi da tabbacin riba, kuma amfani da shi yana buƙatar fahimtar tsaro da haɗarurruka.
