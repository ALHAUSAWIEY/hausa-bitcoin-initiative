Lesson 01: Gabatarwa ga Bitcoin

1. Menene Bitcoin?

Bitcoin wata fasaha ce ta kuɗin dijital wadda ke ba mutane damar aikawa da karɓar kuɗi ta hanyar sadarwa ba tare da buƙatar wani banki ya zama dole a matsayin mai shiga tsakani ba.

An fara gabatar da Bitcoin a shekarar 2008 ta hanyar wata takarda mai suna Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System, wadda Satoshi Nakamoto ya wallafa.

A shekarar 2009 aka fara gudanar da Bitcoin network.

2. Muhimman Abubuwan da Ya Kamata a Fahimta

- Decentralization: Babu wata hukuma guda da ke da cikakken iko a kan Bitcoin network.
- Blockchain: Tsarin bayanai ne da ke adana tarihin transactions.
- Peer-to-Peer: Masu amfani suna iya mu'amala kai tsaye ta hanyar network.
- Cryptography: Ana amfani da dabarun kimiyyar ɓoye bayanai wajen kare mu'amaloli.
- Limited Supply: Adadin Bitcoin da za a taɓa samarwa an iyakance shi zuwa miliyan 21.

3. Bitcoin vs Traditional Banking

A tsarin banki na gargajiya, banki ko wani intermediary yana taka muhimmiyar rawa wajen tabbatar da mu'amaloli.

A Bitcoin, nodes suna tabbatar da bin ƙa'idojin network, yayin da miners ke taimakawa wajen haɗa transactions cikin blocks ta hanyar Proof of Work.

4. Why Bitcoin Matters

Bitcoin ya gabatar da wata sabuwar hanya ta sarrafa kuɗin dijital wadda ba ta dogara da wani babban mai kula da tsarin guda ɗaya ba.

Ya buɗe ƙofa ga sabbin bincike a fannin:

- Digital money.
- Cryptography.
- Distributed systems.
- Financial technology.

5. Key Takeaway

Bitcoin ba kuɗin dijital kawai ba ne; fasaha ce da ta haɗa cryptography, distributed networking, economics, da computer science.
